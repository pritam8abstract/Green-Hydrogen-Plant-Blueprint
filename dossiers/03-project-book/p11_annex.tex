\subsection*{Annex C \textbullet\ Finance words in plain language}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=0pt,itemsep=3pt,font=\color{ink}\bfseries]
\item[SPV] A company created only to own this one plant, so its debts cannot reach the sponsors' other assets.
\item[EBITDA] What the plant earns before paying interest, tax and the wear on its machines.
\item[CFADS] Cash flow available for debt service: the cash actually left to pay the bank each year.
\item[DSCR] CFADS divided by that year's interest plus repayment. 1.30 means thirty paise spare per rupee owed.
\item[LLCR] The same idea over the whole life of the loan, in present-value terms.
\item[IRR] The yearly return that makes all the money in equal to all the money out. Project IRR counts all the capital; equity IRR counts only the owners' share.
\item[IDC] Interest during construction: interest that piles up before the plant earns anything, and gets added to the loan.
\item[MMRA / DSRA] Bank accounts the lenders require: one saves for the stack swap, one holds six months of debt payments.
\item[P50 / P80] The value that half, or 80\%, of simulated outcomes come in below (or, for schedules, finish within).
\item[Real option] The right, but not the duty, to do something later, such as expand or switch technology. It has value because we can wait to see how the world turns out.
\item[Winner's curse] In an auction where everyone guesses the cost, the winner is usually the one who guessed lowest, and so is the one most likely to be wrong.
\end{description}
